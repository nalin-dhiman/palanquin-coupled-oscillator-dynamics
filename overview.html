RESEARCH COMMUNICATION

Rath: a research overview

An introduction to carrying, support timing and the distribution of mechanical loads.

Research purpose

This project's sole purpose is scientific research into collective carrying. The model examines how prescribed shoulder inputs produce motion and contact forces.

Nalin DhimanSchool of Computing and Electrical Engineering

Notes on the implemented model

  • Inputs and contacts, approximately 0:20–1:20. The model prescribes shoulder-support heights and calculates the resulting contact forces. It does not apply measured foot–ground forces. The reference model's two supports represent pole-side resultants, rather than two feet or necessarily two people.
  • Roll equation, approximately 1:40. The equation displayed in the video differs from the reference solver. With left and right body-frame contacts at −a and +a, the implemented moment about the centre of mass is:

    τx = a cos θ (NR − NL) + h sin θ (NL + NR).

    Gravity acts through the centre of mass, so there is no separate gravitational moment about that point. Here θ is the reference model's roll angle; the walking extension uses φ for roll and θ for pitch. The reference solver gives the full contact geometry and equation.
  • Illustrations and interpretation. The architectural sketches and dimension labels are illustrative. The simulator uses assumed geometry and material properties, with vertical movement, roll and pitch as its three body motions. Horizontal travel, yaw, foot dynamics and participants' intentions are outside this model. Numerical values in different video examples correspond to different prescribed inputs.

For reproducible calculations, use the interactive simulation, its parameter exports and the technical guide.
