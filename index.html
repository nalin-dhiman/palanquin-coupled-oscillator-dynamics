What this model represents

A rigid body moves vertically and rotates about its forward axis. Two prescribed supports act through unilateral spring–damper contacts: they can push upward, but cannot pull downward. Different support timing changes the balance between heave and roll.

The optional load-yielding controller adjusts each support using a filtered force signal. It is an assumed controller, not a measured human response. The simulation does not model walking, route choice, pole bending, pitch or cloth dynamics.

The rath is an original schematic. Its dimensions, mass, balance and contact distribution have not been measured. The analytical view shows the assumed effective contacts instead. No decoration or human-body physics is calculated.

Integration uses a fixed-step fourth-order Runge–Kutta method. RMS statistics use the inclusive analysis interval. Contact loss means either computed support force is ≤ 10⁻⁹ N. Change the integration step to assess numerical sensitivity; the displayed work–energy residual is a diagnostic, not empirical validation.
