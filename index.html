Research purpose

This project's sole purpose is scientific research into collective carrying. The simulation evaluates mechanical hypotheses using assumed parameters and prescribed inputs; religious interpretation and participants' experiences require their own evidence.

Model assumptions and interpretation

Walking-informed shoulder inputs. Each carrier is represented by two prescribed shoulder heights. Vertical motion repeats twice per stride, while torso sway repeats once. Cadence and front/rear stride phase are inputs; the body's heave, roll, pitch and contact loads are computed responses.

Comparison modes. Free pitch allows forward–backward tilt. Locked pitch retains four contacts and constrains that rotation. Two resultants average the front/rear inputs before the contact law; individual shoulder loads are then unresolved. The Load redistribution preset demonstrates changing individual forces with negligible angular motion.

Computation and playback. The equations are integrated with a fixed time step, independently of the display frame rate. Pause stops playback and further computation. Parameter changes restart from the initial state. Calculate full run computes the selected duration for inspection and export.

Display controls do not change the results. Camera, speed and motion magnification affect presentation only. The current numerical readouts, graphs, statistics and CSV always use actual values. Magnified views are labelled; force arrows in the contact view use 0.75 mm per newton.

Scientific scope. Dimensions, inertia, compliance and shoulder waveforms are assumed model inputs. Feet, adaptive gait, horizontal forces and travel, yaw, pole bending and learned anticipation are outside the calculation. The illustration provides context rather than measured apparatus geometry.

Numerical accuracy. Compression-only spring–damper contacts can produce force jumps at recontact. Peak-load and zero-force statistics use every integration step in the walking model; traces use the output interval. Assess step-size sensitivity when interpreting contact opening. Numerical consistency and work–energy checks evaluate the calculation; field measurements would be needed for empirical validation. The reference model retains its sampled-statistics definition.
